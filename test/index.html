Wiki-Title-Gen: Seq2Seq Title Generation for Wikipedia · NLP course project [Mar 2025 – Apr 2025]

  • Built GRU encoder–decoder baselines from scratch in PyTorch (bidirectional encoder, teacher forcing, GloVe-300d, hierarchical encoder, beam search). Ablations showed pretrained embeddings helped most, while extra architectural components did not. Q5–Q13
  • Fine-tuned T5-small with Hugging Face Seq2SeqTrainer on 13.4k Wikipedia article–title pairs, reaching 0.86 ROUGE-L on the held-out test set and outperforming every from-scratch RNN variant. Q14–Q18
  • Benchmarked zero-shot prompting on Flan-T5-base and Flan-T5-large (2 prompts × 2 sizes). Flan-T5-large matched fine-tuning at 0.87 ROUGE-L with no training, and prompt wording alone shifted ROUGE-L by 6.6%. Q19–Q22
  • Cut spaCy preprocessing time 3.8× (~60 → 16 min) with batched pipelines, and GRU training time ~20% with fp16 mixed precision via Hugging Face Accelerate. Q4, Q12

How to use this

  • Answer out loud first, then read. The written answer is the target, not a script. Aim to hit its key points in your own words.
  • Treat the red "Be careful" boxes as the highest priority. They mark where a natural-sounding claim would be inaccurate, and exactly what to say instead.
  • Use the review links. Answers link to the matching page in explanations/ for the full derivation.
  • For project questions, lead with numbers and trade-offs. "0.86 ROUGE-L, tied with zero-shot Flan-T5 within about one article on a 100-article test set" is stronger than "it worked really well".
